FitTrack Help Center
Everything you need to get started
1

Logging a Workout

Go to Log → tap an exercise → use the Set Logger to enter reps and weight → Save Set → repeat → tap Finish Workout when done.

2

Finding Exercises

Go to Library → search or tap a muscle group filter → tap "+ Add" on any exercise to add it to your active session.

3

Tracking Personal Records

PRs are detected automatically when you log a heavier set than your previous best. A gold trophy banner fires on the Progress screen.

4

Viewing Your History

Go to History → the calendar highlights all workout days in red. Tap any session card to view full session details.

5

Joining a Fitness Group

Go to Social → tap "+ Create" to start a group or browse existing ones. The feed shows everyone's workouts and PRs in real time.

6

Changing Preferences

Go to Settings → toggle notifications, dark mode, privacy settings, weight units, and rest timer duration.
